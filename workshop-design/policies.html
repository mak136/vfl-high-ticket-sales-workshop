TERMS & CONDITIONS

Workshop registration terms.

These terms apply to registration for the Revenue Incarnate High-Ticket Sales Workshop. By completing a registration or payment, you confirm that the details you provide are accurate and that you are authorised to use the selected payment method.

A registration is confirmed only after payment is successfully verified. We may contact you using the email address or phone number provided to deliver your ticket, joining instructions, event updates, and payment-related support.

The workshop is educational. It provides frameworks, examples, and practical exercises; it does not guarantee commercial outcomes, sales results, or individual business performance.

PRIVACY POLICY

How we use your information.

We collect the contact and registration details needed to process your booking, issue your ticket, provide event access, respond to support requests, and keep appropriate transaction records. This can include your name, email address, phone number, attendance selection, payment status, transaction reference, and any payment screenshot you submit for manual verification.

Payment screenshots are stored privately and made available to authorised staff through a time-limited verification link. They are used only to verify the transfer, maintain transaction records, resolve payment questions, and meet applicable legal or accounting requirements.

We use website analytics and attribution tools to understand site performance and campaign effectiveness. We do not intentionally send your form fields or other personally identifying information to advertising or analytics platforms.

We share information only with service providers needed to operate the workshop, process payments, deliver communications, maintain records, or comply with applicable law. For privacy questions or requests, email billing@revenueincarnate.com.

REFUND POLICY

Registrations are non-refundable.

Workshop registrations are non-refundable once payment has been verified, except where a refund is required by applicable law.

If Revenue Incarnate postpones or reschedules the workshop, your paid registration remains valid for the replacement date. We will send the revised event details to the contact information used at registration. If the workshop is cancelled by Revenue Incarnate and no replacement date is offered, we will contact paid registrants with the available resolution.

RETURN POLICY

There is no physical product to return.

Your purchase is a time-bound workshop registration and may include digital access or materials. These items are not physical goods and cannot be returned. Please review the workshop description, attendance format, ticket price, and any optional add-on before completing payment.

SHIPPING POLICY

Tickets are delivered digitally.

No physical products are shipped. After payment is verified, your ticket and confirmation are sent by email. Online attendees receive joining instructions before the workshop. Onsite attendees receive venue and arrival instructions with their ticket or by email before the event.

Bank-transfer payments may require manual verification before ticket delivery. If you have not received your confirmation, contact billing@revenueincarnate.com.

FAQS

Registration questions.

How is my seat confirmed?+

Your seat is confirmed after payment is verified and your ticket is issued by email.

Can I choose online or onsite attendance?+

Yes. Select Live Online or Live in Karachi when you register. The selected format is recorded with your ticket.

What happens if the event is postponed?+

Your paid registration transfers to the replacement date. Updated information will be sent to the email address used at registration.

How can I get help with payment or my ticket?+

Email billing@revenueincarnate.com during customer-service hours: 11:00 AM–7:00 PM PKT.
