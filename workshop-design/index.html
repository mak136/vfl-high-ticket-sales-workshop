Send me the proposal.

You finish the call thinking it went well. You talked through the service. The buyer asked questions. They seemed interested. So you send the proposal.

Then you spend the next week following up.

I’ve seen this happen in founder and sales-team calls again and again. Sometimes the proposal is fine. The buyer still hasn’t worked through why this needs to change, why your approach fits, what the decision involves, or who else needs to be part of it.

That is difficult to repair with a “just checking in” message.

The three things I want to understand before I pitch

VFL gives a sales conversation a practical order before an offer enters the room.

V01

Vision

Where does the buyer want to get to?

What would actually change in their business if they got there?

CLARITY ONThe outcome they want.
F02

Friction

What is stopping them today?

What have they tried? What is the current situation costing them in money, time, or missed opportunity?

CLARITY ONThe constraint in the way.
L03

Lift

What needs to happen next?

If your offer can help, connect it to the problem you have both identified.

CLARITY ONThe next decision that fits.

The buyer should be able to see why your recommendation fits before you ask them to consider the price.
