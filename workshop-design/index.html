Friction

Who is this workshop for?

People already selling a real offer: founders, agency owners, consultants, salespeople, closers, B2B service providers, and freelancers moving into higher-ticket deals.

I’m a founder. Will this be useful for me?

Yes. The workshop addresses founder-led sales conversations as well as sales roles. VFL focuses on understanding the buyer’s business and helping them reach a clear decision.

Is this relevant to B2B services?

Yes. B2B service providers are a core audience. The framework connects a buyer’s desired business outcome with their constraints and the process your offer provides.

Will I get a script to follow?

The core is Vision, Friction, Lift: a way to reason through a buying decision. It develops diagnosis and commercial judgment instead of relying on memorised closing lines. Any included worksheets or resources will be listed before registration opens.

Should I choose online or Karachi?

Choose online to join the live teaching remotely, or Karachi to attend in the room. The online access instructions and Karachi venue details will be provided to paid attendees. The exact participation format will be published before checkout opens.
